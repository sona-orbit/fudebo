iPhone app for kana handwriting

See the reading.Write the kana with your finger.

Fudebo is only for handwriting. You get the sound and the romaji, you write the character from memory, and the app checks it stroke by stroke. One page a day, about 10 characters.

  • About 3 minutes a page
  • No account
  • Works offline

When a stroke is wrong

The wrong stroke is marked in red

After each character, Fudebo looks at four things: the number of strokes, their order, the direction of each stroke, and its shape and position.

If a stroke is off, that stroke turns red and the correct stroke order plays. You trace the character once, then write it again on your own.

Hints you will see in the app

  • Missing a stroke: this character has 3
  • Stroke 2 goes the wrong way
  • Wrong order: another stroke comes before stroke 3
  • The shape or position of stroke 1 is off

New characters

A new character is written three times

  1. 1

    Trace it

    The full character is in the grid. Trace it in stroke order.

  2. 2

    Follow the faint guide

    The character is now pale. Write over its shadow.

  3. 3

    Write it in an empty box

    Nothing in the grid but the reading. Write it from memory.

Review

Characters you miss come back the next day

A character you got wrong goes into tomorrow's page. One you got right comes back after a longer gap each time.

The kana open one row at a time. When you remember a whole row, the next row opens.

There is no streak counter. Skip a few days, come back and carry on. Nothing is taken away.

A finished page becomes a practice sheet

The characters you wrote that day are laid out in your own handwriting, like a sheet of practice paper.

Details

Content
The 46 basic hiragana. Pro adds katakana, dakuon, handakuon, yōon and 66 basic words.
Each day
One page of about 10 characters. You choose 2, 3 or 5 new characters a day; the rest are reviews.
How you write
With your finger on the screen.
Readings
The iPhone's built-in Japanese voice.
Account
Not needed.
Devices
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. It also runs on iPad.

What it does not do

  • It only practises writing kana. No grammar, kanji or conversation.
  • It checks stroke count, order, direction and shape. It does not grade how nice your handwriting looks.
  • The readings are the iPhone's synthesised voice, not recordings of a person.

Free and Pro

Free

  • The 46 basic hiragana
  • One page a day
  • Ads: a banner at the bottom of the home screen, and at most once a day after a finished page

Pro

  • Katakana
  • Dakuon, handakuon and yōon
  • 66 basic words, written with the kana you have learned
  • Any number of pages a day
  • Practise a row you pick
  • No ads

Monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase, chosen in the app.
