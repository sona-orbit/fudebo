Support

How to use Fudebo, and answers to common questions. If something goes wrong in the app, the quickest way to tell us is “Report a Problem” in Settings; the app version and device model are added for you.

Or send an email:sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

Intro lessons

The first time you open the app there are 4 intro lessons: what kana are, how to read the chart, the three basics of writing, and how to practise. To see them again, tap “Rewatch intro lesson” in Settings.

How a daily page is put together

  • Today's page is counted in writes: 40, about 10 minutes, by default. Tap the amount on the Today's Page card on the home screen and drag to set anything from 10 to 200 (in steps of 10).
  • Characters due for review come first; new ones fill the rest. New characters come “A whole row at a time” by default, or “3 per day”.
  • Each new row starts with a short lesson. When the row is learned, it is shuffled and written from memory once more.
  • The next row opens when you remember nearly all of the row before (at most one not yet steady). Getting your reviews right can open it the same day.
  • The free version has one page a day. With Pro you can keep writing after today's page, or pick a row to practise.

Practice settings

  • How prompts are given: “Write from romaji”, “Listen and write”, “See hiragana, write katakana (also the other way around)”, “Write words from meaning”. You can pick several. The hiragana–katakana swap and words need Pro.
  • Hint: “Faint guide every time”, “Trace first, then remove (recommended)” or “Write from memory right away”.
  • Memory writes per new character: 1 to 3.
  • Grading: “Lenient”, “Standard” or “Strict”, plus “Wrong stroke order counts as a mistake”.
  • Grid: “Cross grid”, “Diagonal grid” or “No grid lines”, plus “Show stroke order numbers while tracing”.

While writing

  • Write with your finger, and lift it after each stroke.
  • Wrote a stroke wrong: tap “Undo stroke”.
  • Can't remember: tap “Hint one stroke” (it draws only the next stroke) or “Show answer”.
  • To hear the reading again, tap “Listen again”.

What happens when a stroke is wrong

The wrong stroke turns red and the correct stroke order plays. You trace the character once, then write it again yourself. If you wrote a similar-looking character, the app tells you which one. That character comes back tomorrow.

Practice

  • The home screen has its own entry for “Practice”, separate from Today's Page. Write for as long as you like.
  • Unlimited writing: first pick a Scope (“Learned”, “Frequently missed”, “One row”, “Choose your own”). A character counts as mastered after 3 correct writes in a row without hints; a mistake resets it, it comes back two or three characters later, and the next time starts with a faint guide. The dots at the top show each character's progress. Stop whenever you like; your progress goes into the review schedule.
  • Practice frequent mistakes: picks the 8 characters you have missed most recently and starts Unlimited writing with them.
  • Look-alike showdown (Pro): pairs such as さ/ち, ぬ/め, シ/ツ and ソ/ン in random order. Get 6 in a row right to clear it.
  • Dictation (Pro): only the sound, no romaji; turn on “Include words in dictation” to include words.
  • Hiragana ↔ Katakana (Pro): see hiragana and write katakana, or the other way around.
  • 60-second challenge (Pro): listen and write; how many can you get right in 60 seconds? Your best score is kept.
  • With the hiragana you have learned, the free version has Unlimited writing and Practice frequent mistakes.

Copybook

Tap a character in the kana chart on the home screen and choose “Write 12 times (copybook)”: 4 boxes to trace, 2 with a faint guide and 6 empty, each one checked. The free version covers the hiragana you have learned; with Pro, every character.

My Copybook

  • Each day's practice sheet is filed by date in “My Copybook”, so you can page back.
  • “Character progress” shows your first, a middle and your latest version of a character side by side. Tap one to replay how you wrote it.
  • Practice sheets and a character's progress can be saved as images to share.

Moving to a new phone

With iCloud on, your handwriting and progress sync to your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new phone, turn on iCloud, and carry on.

Colours in the kana chart

On the home screen, the darker a box, the better you know that character. Tap a character to see its stroke order.

Pro

  • Monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time; the one-time purchase is paid once.
  • On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription: “Manage subscription” in the app's Settings, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Daily reminder

Turn on the daily reminder and pick a time in Settings. If reminders do not arrive, check that notifications for Fudebo are on in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Clearing your history

“Start over (clear learning history)” in Settings deletes the characters you have written, your copybook and your daily stamps. This cannot be undone.
