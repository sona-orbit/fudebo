Support

How to use Fudebo, and answers to common questions. If something goes wrong in the app, the quickest way to tell us is “Report a Problem” in Settings; the app version and device model are added for you.

Or send an email:sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

How a daily page is put together

  • A page has about 10 characters. Characters due for review come first; new ones fill the rest.
  • In Settings you choose 2, 3 or 5 new characters a day.
  • The kana open row by row. When you remember a whole row, the next one opens.
  • The free version has one page a day. With Pro you can keep writing after today's page, or pick a row to practise.

While writing

  • Write with your finger, and lift it after each stroke.
  • Wrote a stroke wrong: tap “Undo stroke”.
  • Can't remember: tap “Hint one stroke” or “Show answer”.
  • To hear the reading again, tap “Listen again”.

What happens when a stroke is wrong

The wrong stroke turns red and the correct stroke order plays. You trace the character once, then write it again yourself. That character comes back tomorrow.

Colours in the kana chart

On the home screen, the darker a box, the better you know that character. Tap a character to see its stroke order.

Pro

  • Monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time; the one-time purchase is paid once.
  • On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription: “Manage subscription” in the app's Settings, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Daily reminder

Turn on the daily reminder and pick a time in Settings. If reminders do not arrive, check that notifications for Fudebo are on in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Clearing your history

“Start over (clear learning history)” in Settings deletes the characters you have written and your daily stamps. This cannot be undone.
