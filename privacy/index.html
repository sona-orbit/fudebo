Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fudebo does not need an account. This page explains when the app sends anything off your phone.

Practice history and handwriting

The characters you wrote, what you got right or wrong, when each one is due again, your daily practice sheets and the handwriting in your copybook are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, they sync to your own iCloud so you can continue on a new phone; they are kept in your iCloud account and the developer cannot see them. The app does not upload them anywhere else. Deleting the app, or tapping “Start over (clear learning history)” in Settings, removes them from the phone.

Reporting a problem

Only when you tap “Report a Problem” in the app's Settings and send it does the app send: your description, an email address if you choose to add one, the app version, system version, device model, interface language, and a random number created when the app was installed. That number is only used to limit how many reports can be sent. Reports are only used to fix problems and to reply to you.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Fudebo requests non-personalised ads only and does not ask for permission to track you. In regions where the law requires consent first (such as the EEA and the UK), Google's consent form appears first; you can change your choice later under “Ad privacy options” in Settings. AdMob still receives the device and network information it needs to show an ad; see Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Pro has no ads.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple. Fudebo never sees your payment details; the app only asks Apple whether you have Pro.

Reminders and readings

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your iPhone. Readings are recordings included in the app and played on the phone. Neither sends any data.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website, not to the app. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Terms of use
