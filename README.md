# Fudebo — Japanese hiragana and katakana handwriting practice for iPhone

Fudebo is an iPhone app for learning to write Japanese kana by hand. You see the reading, write the character from memory with your finger, and the app checks your stroke count, stroke order, direction and shape. It is for beginners who want to actually write hiragana (and, with Pro, katakana), one short page a day, with no account and offline use.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudebo/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6836269334) · [How to use it and FAQ](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudebo/support/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudebo/privacy/)

## Features

- **Stroke-by-stroke checking**: stroke count, order, direction, and shape and position. A wrong stroke turns red and the correct stroke order plays. If you wrote a look-alike (such as ち instead of さ), the app tells you which one.
- **Lessons before you write**: 4 intro lessons, a short lesson for each new row, and a lesson card for every character with stroke order animation, pronunciation tip, writing points, origin (for example あ from 安), look-alikes and example words.
- **Trace, follow a faint guide, then write from memory**: each new character is written three ways before it counts.
- **Spaced review**: characters you miss come back the next day, and correct ones return after longer gaps. There is no streak counter.
- **Practice mode**: unlimited writing until a kana is mastered (3 correct in a row without hints), plus practice for your most frequent mistakes. Pro adds look-alike showdown, dictation, hiragana ↔ katakana and a 60-second challenge.
- **Your own copybook**: "write 12 times" sheets, daily practice sheets filed by date, and first-versus-latest comparisons of your handwriting.
- **Content**: the 46 basic hiragana are free. Pro adds katakana, dakuon, handakuon, yōon and 66 basic words.
- **No account, works offline**, recorded Japanese voice for every kana, and iCloud sync of your handwriting and progress. iPhone with iOS 17 or later; it also runs on iPad.
